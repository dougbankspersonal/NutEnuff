# Nut Enuff: v.2026.09.04

# Overview

Players are squirrels vying to collect and package nuts to earn the most 🪙.

# Gameplay

Three shifts (Day, Afternoon, Night).

A shift has 2 collection phases followed by Packaging and Cleanup.

## A Card Collection Phase

### Fill the conveyor belt

Deal 11 cards from the current Shift Deck face up to the conveyor belt.

### Bidding

Cards are collected through several rounds of bidding.

All players start "In".

For a round of bidding:

- All "In" players secretly indicate how many cards they want to collect (starting from the left end of the belt and proceeding to the right).
- Bids are revealed, and the player with the _lowest_ bid wins: they collect their bid from the left of the belt, movong the cards to their Desk.
  - They are now "Out".
- If more than one player is still "In", add 5 cards to right end of the belt, and repeat with another round of bidding.
- Otherwise, the final "In" player collects all the remaining cards from the belt.

### Ties

If two or more players tie for lowest bid:

- Move the low-bid number of cards from the left of the conveyor belt off to the side: these form the "Pot".
- Each tied player secretly selects whether to "Fight" or "Share": reveal selections simultaneously.
- If all players Share:
  - Enrich: Add 2 cards/player to the Pot from the current deck.
  - Split the Pot: Random. Discard remainder.
- Any players Fight:
  - Sharers get nothing.
  - Fighters split the Pot: Random. Discard remainder.

Players who acquired cards are Out: players who did not acquire cards remain In.

## Package and Cleanup

At the end of a Shift (after 2nd, 4th, and 6th collection rounds):

- Assemble collected nuts into Packages accordinag to the rules of the card: set Packages aside.
- Discard down to **2** cards, if necessary. Each player keeps their own personal discard pile.

# Game End and Final Scoring

After the 3rd round of Packaging, players count 🪙:

- Score 🪙 for completed packages.
- Score 🪙 for Acorns still on the Desk.
- Lose 2 🪙 for each Bad Nut in Discard Pile.
- Lose 1 🪙 for any other cards in Discard Pile.

Most 🪙 wins.
In case of tie, **fewest** packages wins.

## Notes on Specials

- **Raisin**: Use as a simple Peanut, Almond, or a Cashew during packaging.
- **Snack**: As soon as it is acquired, discard it plus two other cards from your Desk (may include other cards just acquired).
- **Double Nuts** count as two instances of the same nut. You cannot use 2 Double Nuts for a package of peanuts (that'd be 4 peanuts, a peanut package must have exactly 3).
