# Overview

Players are squirrels vying to collect and package nuts to earn the most 🪙.

# Gameplay

Three shifts (Day, Afternoon, Night), each shift has 2 collection phases followed by Packaging.

## A Card Collection Phase

### Fill the conveyor belt

Deal 11 cards from the current Shift Deck face up to the conveyor belt.

### Bidding

Cards are collected through several rounds of bidding.

All players start "In".

For a round of bidding:

- All "In" players secretly indicate how many cards they want to collect (starting from the left end of the belt and proceeding to thr right).
- Bids are revealed, and the player with the _lowest_ bid wins: they collect their bid from the left of the belt, and all them to their pile of cards. They are now "Out".
- If more than one player is still "In", add 5 cards to right end of the belt, and repeat with another round of bidding.
- Otherwise, the final "In" player collects all the remaining cards from the belt.

### Ties: Squirrel Cage Match

If two or more players tie for lowest bid, the tied players enter a "Squirrel Cage Match": any players not involved in the tie are bystanders to all this, and are still "In" for later rounds.

Tied cards are removed from the belt: these form the Pot.

Each tied player secretly orients their Cage Match card to "Fight" or "Share". Cards are revealed.

- If all players Shared:
  - Add 3 cards to the Pot.
  - Players have 1 minute to agree on how to divide up the card.
    - If they reach agreement, the cards are divided as agreed.
    - Otherwise the Pot is discarded.
  - The tied players are now all "Out".

- If there's one or more Fight Cards:
  - Any Share players get nothing: they remain 'In'.
  - Any Fight players are now "Out".
  - If there's exactly one "Fight" player they get the Pot. Otherwise the Pot is discarded.

## Cleanup

At the end of a Shift (after 2nd, 4th, and 6th collection rounds) players Package:

- Assemble collected nuts into packages according to the rules of the card: set Packages aside.
- Discard down to 3 cards, if necessary. Each player keeps their own personal discard pile.

# Game End and Final Scoring

After the 3rd round of Packaging, players add up 🪙 scored for completed packages, and lose 1 🪙 for each Bad nut in their discard pile or still in hand.

Most 🪙 wins.

## Notes on packaging

- **Double Nuts** count as two instances of the same nut. You cannot use 2 Double Nuts for a package of peanuts (that'd be 4 peanuts, a peanut package must have exactly 3).
- A package containing at least one **Deluxe Nut** earns one extra 🪙. (A package with 2 or more deluxe nuts does still earns just one extra 🪙).
