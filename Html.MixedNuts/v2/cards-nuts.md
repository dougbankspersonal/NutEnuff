# Overview

Players are squirrels vying to collect and package nuts to earn the most 🪙.

# Gameplay

The game is played over six phases of card collection, followed by a final phase of packaging and scoring.

## A Card Collection Phase

### Deck selection

The phase number determines which deck to use:
1-2: Day
3-4: Afternoon
5-6: Night

### Fill the conveyor belt

Deal 11 cards face up to the conveyor belt.

### Bidding

Cards are collected through several rounds of bidding.

All players start "in".

For a round of bidding:

- All "in" players secretly indicate how many cards they want to collect (starting from the left end of the belt and proceeding to thr right).
- Bids are revealed, and the player with the _lowest_ bid wins: they collect their bid from the left of the belt, and all them to their pile of cards. They are now "out".
- If more than one player is still "in", refill the belt to 11 cards (adding cards to right end of the belt), and repeat with another round of bidding.
- If only one player is still "in", they collect all the remaining cards from the belt plus one random card from the deck.

#### Nil bids

A player may bid "0" to indicate they do not want to collect any cards this round. A "0" bid is not in contention for "lowest bid", and the player who bid "0" is still "in" for the next round of bidding.

If all players bid 0, shuffle the 11 cards back into the deck and refill the belt.

#### Ties: Squirrel Cage Match

If two or more players tie for lowest bid, the tied players enter a "Squirrel Cage Match": any players not involved in the tie are bystanders to all this, and are still "in" for later rounds.

Each tied player draws one of their "Fight" cards at random: they select whether they want to play the "Fight" or "Share" side of the card, and cards are revealed simultaneously.

- If one player chose to "Fight" and their fight number is higher than any other played "Fight" number: that player collects the bid number of cards. They are now "out".
  - Any players who played a weaker "Fight" get nothing and are now "out".
  - Any players who played "Share" get nothing but are still "in".
- If two or more players are tied for the highest "Fight" card:
  - No players collect any cards.
  - Players who played "Fight" are out, players who played "Share" are still in.
- If all players played "Share":
  - Add 3 cards from the deck to the set of bid cards.
  - Players have 60 seconds to agree on how to divide up this pool of cards among them.
    - If they can reach an agreement, the cards are divided as agreed, and all these players are now "out".
    - Otherwise no players get any cards, and all these playes are now "out".

# Game End and Final Scoring

After 6 rounds of card collection, players assemble their collected nuts into packages (according to the type of nut), and score 🪙 as indicated by package type. Most 🪙 wins.

Note that each player may make one "Trail Mix" package, consisting of at most of each type of nut.

## Notes on packaging

**Double Nuts** count as two instances of the same nut. You cannot use a Double Nut in trail mix. You cannot use 2 Double Nuts for a package of peanuts (that'd be 4 peanuts, a peanut package must have exactly 3).
A package containing at least one **Deluxe Nut** earns one extra 🪙.
