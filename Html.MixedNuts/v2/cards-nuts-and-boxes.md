# Overview

Players are squirrels vying to collect and package nuts to earn the most 🪙.

# Card Orientation

Each card has a **Nut** section on top and **Box** section on the bottom.

## Nut

One of **5** types of nut: Peanut, Cashew, Almond, Macadamia, or Pistachio.

## Box

One of **7** types of Box.

A Box has a title (flavor text), a description of how to fill the box, and a reward earned by successfully filling the box at the end of the game.

The description contains one or more numbers, separated by slashes. Each number describes the count of a distinct nut type.

So, for example, if a box description is **3/2**, that means to fill the box you need 5 nuts total: 3 of a kind, and then 2 of a kind different from the 3.

Or, if a box description is **1/1/1/1**, that means you need 4 nuts, all different types.

# Setup

Shuffle the deck.

Arrange player tokens in a queue, left (start) to right (end).

# Gameplay

Over **4** rounds of gameplay players will collect cards which can be used as either nuts or boxes.

When all the collection is finished, players assemble their collected nuts into boxes to score 🪙.

## Collection Rounds

To setup a collection round, deal **11** cards face up to the conveyor belt.

Players then collect cards through a series of bidding phases. All players start "in". Then:

- Each "in" player secretly bids on the number of cards they want to take from the _front_ of the belt. _For example, if Terry bids *3*, he is saying he wants to take the first 3 cards, starting from the left and proceeding to the right._
- Bids are simultaneously revealed. The _lowest_ bid wins: that player collects their bid number of cards from the belt: that player is now "out".
  - The winning player does not "pay" anything: the bid is not an offer of money, it's just a statement of the number of cards the player wants to collect.
  - **IMPORTANT**: The winning player must immediately decide which cards will be used as nuts and which will be used as boxes. They should orient the cards accordingly and sort them into two face down piles in front of them: a pile for nuts and a pile for boxes.
- If there is more than one player still "in":
  - Refresh the belt to contain 11 cards.
  - Do another phase of bidding.
- If there is only one player still "in", they collect the remaining face up cards on the belt, and they are now "out".

Notes:

- A player may bid **0** to say "I don't want any cards this phase": they are excluded from winning, are still "in", and can bid again next round.
- If all players bid **0**, remove the first card from the belt, refill to 11, and repeat.
- If there is a tie for lowest bid, refer to the queue of tokens: the leftmost player wins and moves their token to the end of the queue.
- A player may look through his own facedown piles of nuts and boxes at any time.

# Game End and Final Scoring

Once players have gone through **4** rounds of Collection, each player should have 2 facedown piles: nuts and boxes.

Players then flip their piles, and then fill the boxes (according to box description) as best they can with nuts.

Players then determine a final score:

- Players earn 🪙 as printed on the box for each filled box.
- Unfilled boxes lose one 🪙.
- Leftover nuts lose one 🪙.

If there is a tie, use the following, in order, as tie breakers:

- Fewest leftover nuts.
- Fewest unfilled boxes.
- Most filled boxes.

If there's still a tie, do a foot race.
